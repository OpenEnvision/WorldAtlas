Keystroke nATEr

keystroke_nater reports the normalized rotational absolute trajectory error (nATEr) on constant-action segments. It is the lower-is-better companion to Keystroke Trajectory Score.

nATEr=clamp ⁣(ATErmax⁡(Φtotal,Φmin⁡), 0, 1)(1)\mathrm{nATE}_r = \mathrm{clamp}\!\left(\frac{\mathrm{ATE}_r}{\max(\Phi_{\mathrm{total}}, \Phi_{\min})},\, 0,\, 1\right) \tag{1}nATEr​=clamp(max(Φtotal​,Φmin​)ATEr​​,0,1)(1)

where ATEr\mathrm{ATE}_rATEr​ is mean rotational error in degrees after arc-length resampling and Φtotal\Phi_{\mathrm{total}}Φtotal​ is total recovered rotation (default Φmin⁡=10°\Phi_{\min}=10°Φmin​=10°). Lower is better. Requires an action sidecar plus recoverable camera poses. Backend: pose_action.
