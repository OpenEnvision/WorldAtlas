# Action Control

The **action_control** dimension asks whether a generated rollout follows the control signals declared by the benchmark: text instructions, camera trajectories, structured object constraints, and explicit motion supervision. It aggregates official metric scores into `per_dimension_summary.json` as one of WorldArena's top-level evaluation axes.

This is distinct from perceptual quality, temporal smoothness, or geometric consistency. A prediction can look sharp and fluid while still violating the requested scene, camera path, target object, or prescribed motion. Action control therefore measures **instruction fidelity**—whether displacement and content match what the evaluator was told to produce.

The dimension spans four control families. **Prompt and scene control** is covered by [`prompt_alignment`](../prompt_alignment.md) and [`semantic_alignment`](../semantic_alignment.md). `prompt_alignment` scores protocol anchor frames against the target instruction when explicit supervision exists and the sample is not self-conditioned. **Camera control** uses [`camera_error`](../camera_error.md) to compare predicted camera motion with pose sidecars. **Object control** relies on [`object_detection`](../object_detection.md) over structured object annotations; caption-derived object phrases alone are not scored. **Action and motion control** includes [`motion_accuracy`](../motion_accuracy.md) for mask-supervised dynamic regions, plus the keystroke family—[`keystroke_strict_action_accuracy`](../keystroke_strict_action_accuracy.md), [`keystroke_partial_action_accuracy`](../keystroke_partial_action_accuracy.md), [`keystroke_traj_score`](../keystroke_traj_score.md), [`keystroke_natet`](../keystroke_natet.md), [`keystroke_nater`](../keystroke_nater.md), and [`keystroke_action_score`](../keystroke_action_score.md)—for keyboard-action benchmarks.

WorldArena applies strict eligibility rules throughout this dimension. Missing instruction labels, pose tracks, masks, or structured object annotations yield `not_applicable`, not a forced zero. Read action-control scores together with consistency, physics, and quality metrics: strong appearance alone does not imply that a world model actually obeyed its controls.
